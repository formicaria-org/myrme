# Myrme 0.13.0 — meetings from mail: time ranges, the sender's clock, one meeting a day (contract 1.10)

## What changes for people using formicaria

- **A meeting written as a range gets its time and its end.** "24 November, 14:00–14:30" was read
  as the day with no time; now it is 14:00 to 14:30, read from the email itself.
- **A time is read on the clock of the person who wrote it.** When an email says "14:00" and was
  sent from a clock some hours from yours, the meeting is put at the right moment on your agenda,
  and its box says what was written ("14:00 UTC+05:30"). This needs formicaria 0.63.1, which keeps
  each email's sending clock; older emails keep their time as written.
- **One meeting, not two.** When one email gives a meeting's day in one sentence and its time in
  another, it is proposed once.

## What changes for formicaria

- **Nothing in the contract** (still 1.10). The pass reads the sender's offset from a heading's
  `(sent YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM ±HH:MM)`, the form it already wrote for itself; formicaria 0.63.1 writes it.
