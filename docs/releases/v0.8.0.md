# Myrme 0.8.0 — labels read by the OCR, and a meeting written on another clock moved onto yours (contract 1.7)

## What changes for people using formicaria

- **A packet's label is read by the OCR first.** Photographing a nutrition label now reads it with
  the same OCR that reads printed pages (PP-OCRv6), in well under a second on a laptop.
  - Turned only when upright reads too little: fewer than four figures. Then the other three turns
    are tried, and the one with the most figures is kept.
  - A vision model reads the label only when the OCR found too little, and only one fit for labels.
  - **A phone can read labels** too, through the OCR it already has for pages.
  - Measured on thirty packets against figures checked by hand: 155 of 210 figures right and 14
    wrong, in 0.67 s, against 85–88 right in 28–40 s for the vision models
    (`docs/research/2026-10-20-packet-labels.md`).
- **A meeting time given on another clock is moved onto yours.** An email that says "Tuesday 27th at
  10am Berlin time" is proposed at 18:00 on a Tokyo agenda — Berlin leaves summer time on 25
  October, so the conversion is done on the meeting's own day. Before, it was proposed at 10:00.
  - The zone must be written right after the time: a place and "time" ("New York time", "ora
    italiana"), an abbreviation (CET, SGT, PT), or an offset (UTC+2). A time with no zone after it
    is taken as yours, as before.
  - The assistant's box keeps the email's own words: *18:00 (10:00 Berlin time)*.
  - Every conversation is read once more, so a meeting already proposed at the wrong clock is
    proposed moving.
  - "Today" is your zone's day, no longer UTC's.

## What changes for formicaria

- **Contract 1.7** (an addition): `label::{read_with, ready_json_with}` and `ocr::{shared,
  ReadPage}`. A formicaria built for 1.6 still links this release.
- The meeting pass reads the person's zone from formicaria's existing `/api/calendars` — `zone`
  (formicaria 0.15.0), or `offset` from an older one — else the computer's own. Nothing to do.
- A new dependency, `jiff` (MIT OR Unlicense), the zone database.

## Checked

- Unit tests: zones read after a time, and words that name no clock; the conversion across the 25
  October change; a conversion that moves the day; your zone as a name or an offset.
- The meeting eval: three new cases (`zone-berlin`, `zone-new-york-next-day`,
  `zone-unwritten-stays`); every recorded model answer on the seventeen others scores exactly as
  before.
- The label OCR: thirty packets, as above.
- `pixi run ci` and `pixi run integration` pass.
