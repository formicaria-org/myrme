# Myrme 0.9.0 — the meeting pass reads only what is new (contract 1.7)

## What changes for people using formicaria

- **A long email exchange gets its next meeting proposed.** In a conversation with a meeting a week,
  the assistant used to read every email again at each new one and list every meeting it had ever
  fixed. Its answer outgrew its limit, was cut, and was taken for "no meetings": the next meeting was
  never proposed, and the conversation was not looked at again until another email came.
- **The assistant now reads only what is new**: the emails since its last read, the email they
  answer, and the earlier emails that still fix a meeting to come or ask a question still open. The
  rest of the conversation is one line. It is faster on every later email of a long exchange.
- **A cut or repeating answer is read again**, with fewer emails, instead of being taken for
  "nothing". An email too long to read even alone is passed over, and the mail panel says so.
- **"See you on the 30th, same link"** is the meeting already on the 30th, not a move to the whole day.
- **After updating, each mail conversation is read once more**, in steps for a long one. A meeting
  already proposed, accepted or rejected is not asked again.

## What changes for formicaria

- Nothing to do. The contract stays **1.7**: no `/api` call, catalogue key or note convention changed.
- The pass's own memory file (`mail-meetings.json` in the assistant's runtime folder) gains a
  `ledger` per conversation and moves to version 8, so every conversation is read again once.

## Checked

- Unit tests with scripted models: a long exchange read in steps; a cut answer and a repeating answer
  read again with fewer emails; a new email read with only what is still in play; a changed email
  that has already been read means reading from the start; a question kept in view until someone
  else answers; the pass proposing the next meeting once, then reading the reply in one call.
- The meeting eval, 21 cases with a new long exchange (`weekly-long`), qwen3-vl-4b held to the schema:
  20/21 read window by window, as the pass reads, and 20/21 read whole. The miss is the known
  `question-declined`.
- The real exchange that prompted this was checked locally and is not in the repository: read whole
  it lost the meeting; read window by window it found it.
