# Myrme 0.7.1 — the mail verdict in plain words, and a meeting that clashes says so (contract 1.6)

## What changes for people using formicaria

- **The mail panel's sentences are plain words.** For example:
  - "no change — the meeting is still on Fri 23 Oct at 16:00";
  - "no change — the meeting on Fri 16 Oct at 16:30 has taken place", for a meeting already held
    when the email came, where it used to say the meeting "stays at" a time already gone;
  - "one more possible meeting was not proposed: its words are not in the emails", where it said
    "left out: not found in the emails as written".

  A conversation already read keeps its old sentence until its next email.
- **Two meetings at once are said.** When a meeting the assistant keeps or proposes starts less than
  an hour from another meeting, it says so: "…but it clashes with the meeting of "Mei Lin …", Fri
  23 Oct at 16:00".
  - The note proposed carries a **Two meetings at once** line, seen before it is accepted.
  - The other meeting can be another conversation's, one linked to it, or any meeting in the
    calendar.

## What changes for formicaria

Nothing to do: the same contract (1.6), and no new key. formicaria 0.9.8 already marks clashes in
its calendar by the same one-hour measure.

## Checked

- Unit tests:
  - dates in words;
  - a clash within the hour, not at an hour, and not with the conversation's own meeting;
  - a linked meeting and any other meeting in the calendar;
  - the kept-meeting sentence with its clash, and a meeting already held.
- `pixi run ci` and `pixi run integration` pass.
