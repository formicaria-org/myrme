# Myrme 0.10.0 — the assistant follows formicaria's clock (contract 1.8)

## What changes for people using formicaria

- **The assistant's times are formicaria's.** It used to fall back on its own computer's zone when
  formicaria did not name one (always, on the phone) and to count "today" itself. After a change of
  zone, the meetings it found in mail could come out hours off, or a meeting already proposed could
  be proposed again. It now takes the zone and the day from formicaria's clock (Settings → Clock,
  formicaria 0.19), and when there is none it reads no mail for meetings instead of guessing.
- **A meeting is proposed as the moment it is**, so a clock that changes between the assistant
  reading an email and formicaria writing the meeting cannot move it.
- **"Tomorrow" in an email is the sender's tomorrow.** An email written late in the evening in
  another zone is already the next day on yours; the day it names is counted from the sender's day.
- **A chat knows what day it is**, on formicaria's clock, so "next Monday" has an anchor.
- **Only a device that reads mail looks for meetings in it**, so two computers on one notebook do
  not both propose the same meeting.

## What changes for formicaria

- **Contract 1.8**, an addition: `VaultAccess::clock()`, a provided method answering `None`. The
  desktop's `FmServe` asks `/api/clock` (formicaria 0.19); the phone's vault may answer it in process.
  A formicaria without `/api/clock` keeps working: the zone `/api/calendars` names is used, with
  naive starts, as before.
- When the clock answers `instants: true`, `start`/`due` in `propose_with`/`propose_note` are RFC 3339
  moments (`2026-11-02T16:30:00+09:00`); formicaria 0.19 converts them onto the vault's clock.
- Headings written with their offset (`### Sender — 2026-10-07 22:30 -05:00`, planned for formicaria
  0.20) are read; headings without one are read as before.
- The pass's memory file keeps what it proposed as instants as well (`proposed_at`); its version
  stays 8, so nothing is read again for this update.

  and starts sent as moments; what was proposed remembered across a change of zone, and a memory
  from before read on the clock in force; "tomorrow" counted from the sender's day (red without the
  change); a chat told the day, an edit not, and nothing said with no clock.
- `pixi run clock`, new in `pixi run ci`: no source asks the system for its zone or local time
  (shown to fail on such a call).
- The meeting eval, 23 cases with two new ones whose headings carry offsets, qwen3-vl-4b held to the
  schema, read window by window as the pass reads: 22/23. The miss is the known `question-declined`.
- `pixi run ci` and `pixi run integration`, against the pinned formicaria (no `/api/clock`: the
  fallback holds) and against formicaria 0.19 (`FM_CHECKOUT`): a zone chosen in its Settings → Clock
  is the one the assistant reads, with its `today` and `instants`.
- `FM_CHECKOUT` integration runs now build into `.fm/checkout-target`: they had replaced the
  checkout's own `target/release/fm-serve`, the binary formicaria's desktop launcher runs.
