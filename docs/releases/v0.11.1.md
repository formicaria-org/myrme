# Myrme 0.11.1 — meetings on the right clock when the zone is misspelt; research that quotes truly (contract 1.9)

## What changes for people using formicaria

- **A meeting whose zone is misspelt lands at the right time.** "17:30 Melbuorne time" was read as
  17:30 on your own clock. A place one slip away (a letter missing, extra, changed, or two swapped)
  is now read wherever a clock is meant — before "time", after "ora di" or "heure de" — when exactly
  one place is that close. "Standard / daylight / summer time" after a place is read too.
- **An invitation's zone confirms the time.** When the sentence you wrote names no zone, the same time
  in the email's invitation line ("Time: Nov 4, 2026 06:00 PM Tokyo") gives it. A place after a time
  outside an invitation is not read: it is often an address.
- **`/research` searches arXiv for every word**, not any of them: "vitamin K deficiency" found 642,502
  papers, a physics paper first. Filler words (how, does, in…) are left out, or a question found
  nothing.
- **A research claim keeps to its quote.** A claim is checked only against the text the model was
  shown; one whose number or "not" is not in its quote is dropped; and a claim or source title reaches
  your note as plain text, with no link but the citation's.
- **The web is asked politely.** The assistant names itself and the project to the sources it asks;
  an answer is reused for twelve hours; a source that says it is busy is left alone until it says
  when to come back.

## What changes for formicaria

- **Nothing in the contract.** Same version, 1.9.
- The desktop search proxy (`agents/search-proxy.py`) sends an honest User-Agent; its DuckDuckGo
  engine scrapes HTML and may now be refused (it is fail-soft).

  "local time", two slips, a slip as near one place as another); the meeting pass end to end with a

  User-Agent; quotes checked against what was shown; claims as plain text; numbers and negations;
  the answer cache and `Retry-After`.
- **Recorded answers** of four model variants re-scored: the claim check dropped two claims, both

  CPU: 5/8 exact, 8/8 grounded, with and without it.
- **The zone reader over real mail, locally** (nothing copied into the repository): no false reading.
