# Making Myrme faster (research and measurements, 2026-10-19)

> **Status: paused on 2026-10-19 at the owner's request; resumes from §5.** What is below was
> measured or verified; nothing is adopted yet. A measure is adopted by a dated entry in
> `docs/context/decisions.md`.

**The owner:** *"research methods that can make myrme faster: for instance dedicated runtimes for
edge devices? Other measures?"*, then *"it is really slow"* on the phone, *"do a thorough search and
benchmark"*, *"consider also the custom kernels like the one from Arm"*.

**How.** Four research agents (runtimes and backends; model-side measures; system-level measures in
our stack; the Dimensity 7300 class specifically), about 550k tokens in all, each claim with its
source. Then measurements on our own hardware with `pixi run eval-speed`: the method evals, with
llama-server's own timings per request. Phone runs go over USB (`EVAL_ADB_DIR`), one at a time, on
the device people use.

**Hardware.**
- **Phone:** Xiaomi 24095PCADG, MediaTek Dimensity 7300 (MT6878): 4× Cortex-A78 + 4× A55, Armv8.2
  with dotprod, no i8mm or SME; Mali-G615 MC2; Android 16. Runtime b10081 + llguidance.
- **Laptop:** Intel i7-11800H (8 cores, AVX-512), RTX 3050 Laptop 4 GB. Runtime b10076 + llguidance.

## 1. Where the time goes (measured)

### Phone, lfm2.5-vl-450m Q8_0, 4 threads, ctx 4096 (the shipped phone setup)

| method | requests | prompt tok | prefill tok/s | prefill ms | gen tok | decode tok/s | s/request |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| meetings | 34 | 77 | 306 | 253 | 93 | 23 | 4.3 |
| research | 8 | 110 | 360 | 306 | 137 | 24 | 6.0 |
| edits | 14 | 111 | 367 | 302 | 16 | 26 | 0.9 |
| recipe | 60 | 141 | 389 | 361 | 315 | 23 | 14.0 |
| image (handwriting) | 30 | 1926 | 102 | 18798 | 441 | 21 | 38.8 |

- **Text: reading is fast, writing is the cost.** First token in 0.25–0.36 s; the answer comes at
  23–26 tok/s, so a recipe's ~315 tokens take ~13 s.
- **Photos: the vision encoder is the cost.** A 4128×2322 page becomes 9 tiles; each takes ~1.55 s to
  encode, so ~14 s of the ~19 s "prefill" is encoding (measured with `llama-mtmd-cli`, §2.1).
- `llama-bench`: pp512 466, pp2048 417, tg64 37 tok/s. The CPU variant loaded is
  `libggml-cpu-android_armv8.2_2.so` (dotprod; repacked kernels active).
- Threads: 2 → 250, **4 → 448**, 6 → 359, 8 → 364 tok/s prefill. The shipped `threads_mobile = 4`
  is right.
- The eval cases are short (≈100 prompt tokens). Real notes are 1–6k tokens; see `llama-bench` rows.

### Laptop, qwen3-vl-4b Q4_K_M

`llama-bench` (prompt reading at 512 and 4096 tokens, writing):

| setup | pp512 | pp4096 | tg64 |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPU only (`-dev none`, AVX-512 variant) | 97 | **70** | 15 |
| Vulkan runtime, layers on CPU (large batches still sent to the GPU) | 827 | 749 | 14 |
| RTX 3050, all layers on GPU | 1575 | **1264** | 43 |

On a laptop without a usable GPU, a 4000-token note waits ~57 s for the first word; on the RTX 3050,
~3 s.

Method evals, all layers on the RTX 3050 (projector on CPU, as the catalogue sets):

| method | requests | prompt tok | prefill tok/s | prefill ms | gen tok | decode tok/s | s/request |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| meetings | 34 | 73 | 709 | 103 | 69 | 43 | 1.7 |
| research | 8 | 104 | 831 | 126 | 97 | 42 | 2.4 |
| edits | 14 | 102 | 805 | 127 | 31 | 41 | 0.9 |
| recipe | 33* | 204 | 898 | 228 | 278 | 40 | 7.2 |
| image | 5* | 1430 | 47 | 30617 | 456 | 40 | 42.0 |

\* Incomplete: the run overlapped a full temporary disk and a build. The image row still shows the
point: with the projector on the CPU, encoding dominates on the laptop too.

**Correction.** A first "CPU" table was measured with the Vulkan runtime and no layers offloaded.
llama.cpp then still ships large batches to the GPU, copying weights each time, which made short
prompts take ~2 s. It is not a CPU-only number and is not used here.

## 2. Phone experiments

### 2.1 Page photos: tiles and projector (measured)

One real page (4128×2322), `llama-mtmd-cli`, 4 threads:

| page size | projector | image tiles | encode per tile | encode total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| original | F16 | 9 | 1.55 s | ~14 s |
| 1024 px long side | F16 | 1 | 1.53 s | **1.5 s** |
| original | Q8_0 | 9 | 1.84 s | ~16.5 s |
| 1024 px long side | Q8_0 | 1 | 1.82 s | 1.8 s |

- **Fewer tiles is the large win: ~9× less encoding.** It is a deliberate input size, not the silent
  downscale `openai.rs` rules out. llama.cpp already shrinks every photo to at most 10 tiles.
- **The Q8_0 projector is ~18% slower than F16 on this CPU**, which has FP16 arithmetic. Keep F16.

**Accuracy and time against page size** (handwriting eval, 30 pages, on the phone):

| page size | prompt tokens | prompt time | answer tokens | answer time | mean WER |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| original (4128 px) | 1926 | 18.4 s | 429 | 19.2 s | 96.3% |
| 1536 px | 1926 (same tiles) | 19.2 s | 573 | 25.9 s | 82.7% |
| 1024 px (1 tile) | **374** | **3.3 s** | **940** | 38.9 s | 96.2% |

- One tile cuts the page's prompt time from 18.4 s to 3.3 s. But with less detail the 450M model
  loops: 18 of 30 answers run past 900 tokens, which costs more than the saving.
- **Repetition control (llama.cpp's DRY sampler, multiplier 0.8) is not the fix:** answers shorten
  (mean 576 tokens) but turn into varied nonsense, WER 254.6% (laptop GPU, 1024 px).
- The phone model cannot read handwriting at any size, so handwriting cannot decide the tile
  question. **A typed-page eval** was built for it: 30 Wikibooks recipe texts (CC BY-SA) rendered as
  photo-sized pages (`~/bench-scratch/typed-cases.json`, not yet in the repo). At full size the
  450M reads them at **49.0% WER** (1926 prompt tokens, 560 answer tokens). The 1-tile run was
  stopped before it finished.

### 2.2 Quant formats and kernels (measured on the phone, `llama-bench -t 4`, tok/s)

| model | build | read 512 | read 2048 | write |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 450M **Q8_0** (shipped) | release | 452 | 414 | 37 |
| 450M Q8_0 | KleidiAI build | **702** | **595** | 39 |
| 450M **Q4_0** | release | 494 | 443 | **62** |
| 450M Q4_0 | KleidiAI build | 458 | 425 | 60 |
| 450M **Q4_K_M** | release | 397 | 361 | **59** |
| 450M Q4_K_M | KleidiAI build | 397 | 363 | 60 |
| 1.2B Q4_0 | release | 147 | 141 | 20 |
| 1.2B Q4_0 | KleidiAI build | 148 | 138 | 20 |
| 1.2B Q4_K_M | release | 116 | 105 | 19 |

- **4-bit weights write ~60% faster than Q8_0** on this phone (62/59 vs 37 tok/s). Writing is
  where text methods spend their time here (§1), so this is the largest text speed-up measured.
- **The KleidiAI build speeds up only Q8_0 reading (+44–55%).** That build was also compiled for
  this CPU's exact features (`-march=armv8.2-a+dotprod+fp16`), unlike the release's runtime-chosen
  variant, so the gain may be the flags rather than KleidiAI. **A control build (same flags,
  KleidiAI off) is built and on the phone but not yet run** (§5).

### 2.3 Accuracy of the candidates (same evals, laptop RTX 3050)

Accuracy does not depend on the hardware, so it was measured on the laptop while the phone measured
speed.

| model | meetings exact | research exact / grounded | edits exact | recipe exact / ingredients found / invented |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 450M Q8_0 (shipped) | 5/17 | 5/8 / 4/8 | 0/7 | 8/30 / 102 of 257 / 18 |
| 450M Q4_0 | 5/17 | 5/8 / 4/8 | 0/7 | 7/30 / **59** / 18 |
| 450M **Q4_K_M** | 5/17 | 6/8 / 4/8 | 0/7 | 9/30 / 89 / 16 |
| 1.2B Q4_0 | **13/17** | 5/8 / **7/8** | **3/7** | 3/30 / **157** / 25 |
| 1.2B Q4_K_M | **13/17** | **7/8** / 6/8 | **3/7** | *stopped* |

- **450M Q4_K_M matches Q8_0 on accuracy and writes ~60% faster:** the front-runner for a free
  speed-up on the phone. Q4_0 is as fast but loses recipe ingredients.
- **The shipped phone model is weak at the methods themselves** (0/7 note edits, 5/17 meetings).
  The 1.2B is far better (13/17, 3/7) and about 3× slower. That is a product choice, not only a speed
  one: a smaller model that answers wrongly is not faster for the person.

### 2.4 The Mali GPU (Vulkan)

- The GPU reports subgroup size 16, 32 KB shared memory, **no integer dot product and no matrix
  cores**.
- **Upstream Vulkan computes wrong results on it:** `test-backend-ops` failed 25 of 982 matmul cases
  (error ≈1.0, i.e. unrelated output), the known subgroup-16 bug (llama.cpp #28637).
- **The fix from llama.cpp PR #30106 (unmerged), ported to b10081, passes 982/982**, and about
  11,000 further cases across all ops passed with no failure before the suite was stopped. The port
  is `~/bench-scratch/keep/builds/mali-subgroup16-fix-pr30106-port.patch` (not in the repo).
- **Speed on the GPU is not measured yet** (§5). Shipping it would mean carrying that patch in
  Myrme's runtime build until upstream merges it.

## 3. What the research says

### 3.1 In our own stack (largest expected gains, least effort)

- **Prompt layout defeats the prompt cache.** llama-server reuses only the longest common token
  prefix; every method starts with its own system prompt.
  - The meeting pass sends up to ~6500 tokens of email twice (meetings, then title) under different
    system prompts, so the second call reads it all again (`fm-agent-run/src/meetings.rs`).
  - Edits put history before the note, so the note's cache is lost every turn
    (`fm-agent/src/lib.rs`, `assemble_turn_context`).
  - The latest message is sent twice: once in the history, once as the ask.
  - Fix: `[shared system][document][history][method instruction + ask]`, with the instruction as its
    own last user message. On hybrid models (LFM2) a context checkpoint then lands after the document.
- **`--cache-reuse` cannot help:** llama-server switches it off whenever a projector is loaded,
  which is always our case (b10076 source). One report recommended it; the source decides.
- **Flags worth an A/B:** drop `--no-warmup`; `-np 1` (our client is sequential); an explicit
  `-cram` (the 8 GiB default host prompt cache is not counted by our memory admission); `-ub` on the
  laptop GPU; `-ctk/-ctv q8_0` on the 4 GB GPU. Flash attention is already `auto`.
- **llguidance costs little:** about 50 µs per token; grammars are rebuilt per request but our
  schemas are small.
- **Bug found and fixed:** the phone started its model with the global 2048-token window instead of
  the model's 4096 (Myrme 0.5.1).

### 3.2 Runtimes and kernels for the phone

- **The A78 has dotprod only.** llama.cpp's dotprod repacking (Q4_0, Q4_K, Q6_K, Q8_0) is already in
  b10081 and active. **KleidiAI's** large gains need i8mm or SME2; on this CPU it is measured in §2.2.
- **Mali GPU via llama.cpp Vulkan:** no published number on a Mali-G615 MC2. Flagship Malis reach
  126–205 tok/s prefill, below our CPU's 466. Open bugs give wrong matmul results on subgroup-16
  Malis (llama.cpp #28637, fix #30106 open). Built here; measured only after `test-backend-ops`
  passes on the phone.
- **LiteRT-LM (Google):** the only engine with fast published Mali numbers (ML Drift: 500–800 tok/s
  prefill on a 7-core Mali-G715 for 2–3B models). It does JSON-Schema output (LLGuidance) but has
  Mali bugs, its own model format, and no LFM2/LFM2.5 build. It is built and measured on CPU and GPU
  in §2.2.
- **NPU:** no public path runs a 0.5–1.5B LLM on the 7300's APU. Prebuilt LLM files exist only for
  the Dimensity 9300/9400.
- **MNN, MLC, ExecuTorch:** no Mali numbers worth the switch; MNN reports Mali problems.
- **Do not upgrade llama.cpp blindly:** vision encoding on Android CPU is ~2.7× slower on master than
  on b10516 (#27649, closed without a fix). Measure encode time before any runtime upgrade.

### 3.3 Laptops

- An optional **CUDA** runtime for NVIDIA users; one undated third-party report puts Vulkan prefill
  ~3× below CUDA. The CUDA libraries are under NVIDIA's EULA, so it would be an opt-in download.
- On Apple M5 with macOS 26.2 or later, Metal 4's tensor path gives 2–3× prompt processing (user
  report); b10076 has it.
- **A smaller default without a GPU** (§1: 70 tok/s on CPU for the 4B model).

### 3.4 Models

- **Handwriting:** Qwen3.5-2B (Apache-2.0) reports OCR scores at or above Qwen3-VL-4B, at half the
  parameters (vendor numbers).
- **Speech, Italian:** Qwen3-ASR-0.6B (Apache-2.0, runs in llama.cpp) and Parakeet-TDT-0.6B-v3
  (CC-BY-4.0) report far lower Italian WER than whisper base or small (vendor numbers).
- **Task-tuned small models:** LoRA-tuned 0.8–2B models reach near parity on narrow extraction
  tasks in the literature. Not for research with citations, where no ≤2B model quoted verbatim in
  our bench.
- **Speculative decoding** works with llguidance (the target verifies each drafted token under the
  grammar), but only speeds up writing. The n-gram modes need no extra model and suit copy-heavy
  edits.
- **Licence:** the phone default (LFM2.5, LFM Open License v1.0) limits commercial use to entities
  under $10M annual revenue. It is shown honestly before download; Apache-2.0 alternatives of similar
  size exist (Qwen3.5-0.8B, Granite-4.0-1B). The owner's decision.

## Mistakes in this session, kept so they are not repeated

- A "CPU" table measured with the Vulkan runtime was not CPU-only (§1, *Correction*).
- `pkill -f` / `pgrep -f` with a pattern that also appeared in the calling shell matched that shell:
  once it killed the shell, once a wait loop never ended and the phone sat idle about 50 minutes.
  Check a PID's command line before stopping it.
- Large downloads went to `/tmp`, which is a 7.5 GB RAM disk here, and filled it.
